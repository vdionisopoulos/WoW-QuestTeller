1. Scope

About this policy

Alisander: Chronicles of the Forsaken is an independent World of Warcraft fan-made narrative website published using Blogger. This Privacy Policy applies to this website and explains the categories of information that may be processed through hosting, analytics, comments, contact communications and, if enabled in the future, advertising.

The website is operated independently and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.

2. Information

Information that may be collected

Information you provide

If you contact the site, leave a comment where comments are available, or otherwise submit information voluntarily, the information you provide may include your name, email address, message, account display name and any other information you choose to include.

Technical and usage information

When you visit the site, Blogger, Google and other service providers used by the site may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, referring pages, approximate location derived from network information, pages viewed, timestamps and interactions with the site.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies, local storage and similar technologies may be used by Blogger, Google Analytics and, if advertising is enabled, advertising services. More information is available in the Cookie Policy.

3. Purposes

Why information is used

Information may be processed for purposes including:

  • operating, securing and maintaining the website;
  • displaying requested pages and site functionality;
  • understanding readership, traffic and site performance;
  • detecting abuse, spam, fraud or technical problems;
  • responding to legitimate enquiries and privacy requests;
  • improving navigation, accessibility and content;
  • complying with applicable legal obligations; and
  • serving and measuring advertising if advertising is enabled in the future.
4. Analytics

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use the site. Google Analytics may process device, browser, network and interaction information and may use cookies or similar technologies depending on configuration, region and consent choices.

Analytics information is used to understand aggregate readership and improve the site, rather than to identify visitors personally.

Learn more about Google's privacy practices at Google's Privacy Policy and about how Google uses information from sites and apps at Google's information for partner sites and apps .

Google AdSense and advertising technologies

The site may use Google AdSense or other Google advertising services in the future. When advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, local storage or similar technologies to deliver, limit, measure and report advertising, subject to applicable law and consent choices.

Advertising may be personalized where permitted and where the required consent has been obtained. In other circumstances, non-personalized or limited advertising may be used depending on configuration, region and applicable requirements.

Where Google requires a certified Consent Management Platform (CMP), the site will use an appropriate consent mechanism before serving advertising that requires such consent.

Legal bases where applicable

Depending on your location and the activity involved, information may be processed because it is necessary to provide the site you requested, because there is a legitimate interest in operating and securing the site, because consent has been provided for optional technologies, or because processing is required by law.

Where consent is the applicable basis, you may withdraw that consent through the available consent controls or browser settings, subject to the limitations of those tools.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the site, including Google services used for Blogger hosting, analytics, security and, if enabled, advertising.
