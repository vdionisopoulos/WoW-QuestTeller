Arc I — The Awakening

Alisander's first arc is not simply the story of a Forsaken leaving a graveyard.

It is the story of a dead man discovering what remains after death has stripped away everything familiar.

His body returned first.

His memories returned only in fragments.

His magic returned almost immediately.

Everything else has required investigation.

Current Record

Deathknell

Deathknell remains the center of Alisander's known world.

It is where he awakened.

Where he discovered that his magic had survived.

Where he first encountered the Mindless.

Where he watched other newly risen Forsaken accept, reject or flee from their second existence.

Where he helped gather the dead who might become the next generation of Forsaken.

And where he first learned that being undead and being Scourge are not the same thing.

It is also where he confronted Lilian Voss with the truth of her undead reflection and discovered that showing someone reality does not grant the right to decide what they must do with it.

Alisander has now travelled beyond Deathknell's immediate graves, fought enemies in the surrounding forests and returned to face a more difficult problem inside the settlement itself: the boundary between truth, acceptance and coercion. Deathknell remains his current base and Arc I — The Awakening — is still in progress.

When the story genuinely moves beyond Deathknell, this location will become Visited rather than Current, and the next stage of the Journey will begin.
