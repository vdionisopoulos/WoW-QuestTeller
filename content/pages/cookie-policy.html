1. Overview

What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by a website or service in your browser. Websites may also use local storage, device identifiers, pixels and similar technologies. These tools can support essential functionality, security, analytics, preferences and advertising.

2. Categories

Contact form anti-spam storage

After a successful Contact form submission, this site stores a timestamp in your browser's local storage for approximately five minutes. The timestamp is used only to discourage repeated submissions from the same browser and is not used for advertising, analytics profiling or cross-site tracking.

3. Blogger

Platform cookies

This site is hosted on Blogger, a Google service. Blogger and Google may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for platform functionality, security, account features, abuse prevention and service delivery.

4. Analytics

Google Analytics 4

Google Analytics 4 is used to understand aggregate traffic and how visitors interact with the site. Analytics technologies may collect information such as page views, session information, referral sources, device/browser characteristics and approximate location derived from network information.

Google Analytics commonly uses first-party cookies whose names can include _ga and cookies beginning with _ga_. Exact technologies, names and retention behavior can change as Google updates its services and as site configuration changes.

Learn more at How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services .

The site may enable Google AdSense in the future. 6. Consent

Consent-management controls

In regions where consent is required for optional cookies or advertising technologies, the site should present an appropriate consent-management interface. For Google advertising in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google requires qualifying publishers to use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework where the requirement applies.

Consent settings may allow you to accept, reject or manage available purposes and vendors. The precise options depend on the services enabled on the site and your region.

7. Your Controls

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block all cookies, block third-party cookies, clear stored cookies or receive warnings before cookies are stored. Blocking essential technologies may affect site functionality.

Where a consent-management interface is provided on this site, use that interface to change the choices it makes available. You may also review Google's advertising controls at My Ad Center .
