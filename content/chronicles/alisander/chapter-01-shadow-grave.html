Deathknell — Tirisfal Glades

The Awakening

The first thing Alisander remembered was the cold.

Not the clean cold of winter wind or mountain snow. This cold lived inside him. It clung to his bones. It filled the hollow spaces where warmth had once belonged.

He opened his eyes beneath a sky the color of old iron and found himself staring through the crooked branches of dead trees. Their black limbs twisted above him like the fingers of something enormous buried beneath the earth.

For several long seconds, he simply lay there.

No breath came.

Then he realized he did not need one.

Alisander sat upright. The movement felt wrong. His joints protested with dry, brittle clicks. His skin had lost the warmth of life, stretched pale and thin over a body that no longer obeyed the rules he remembered.

He raised one hand before his face.

Long fingers. Gray flesh. No pulse.

And yet...

Thought. Memory. Will.

He was dead. The troubling part was that death had apparently failed to stop him.

Deathknell

Around him, Deathknell stirred beneath the endless twilight of Tirisfal.

Figures moved among the graves and ruined buildings—Forsaken like himself, some newly awakened and confused, others already carrying themselves with the grim purpose of those who had accepted what they had become.

Alisander watched them carefully.

A warrior tested the edge of a rusted blade. Another corpse dragged itself from the soil with considerably less dignity. Farther up the hill, an undertaker stood waiting beside a battered structure overlooking the graveyard.

Undertaker Mordo.

There was something unsettling about how casually the Forsaken spoke of resurrection.

Or perhaps resurrection was the wrong word.

This was not life restored.

This was something else.

A continuation. A refusal. An insult directed at the grave itself.

Alisander found that thought strangely comforting.

Fragments of a Former Life

There were holes in his memory.

He remembered books. Candles. Ink staining his fingers.

He remembered the delicate architecture of spellcraft—how a single misplaced syllable could turn fire into catastrophe.

He remembered power gathering between his hands.

Arcane energy.

Beautiful. Precise. Hungry.

But his final moments remained buried beneath a wall of darkness.

There had been screaming. That much survived.

A city? A village? The dead walking through streets?

He reached deeper into the memory and found only pain.

Then nothing.

Alisander lowered his hand.

Perhaps the man he had been was gone.

Perhaps that was for the best.

Mordo's Request

Undertaker Mordo studied him with the detached interest of a craftsman examining something recently repaired.

The newly risen required supplies.

Embalming fluid. Corpse-stitching twine.

Practical necessities for bodies that had inconveniently begun moving again.

The materials could be found inside the nearby Shadow Grave.

Darnell would accompany him.

It was almost absurd.

Alisander had awakened from death and, within minutes, had been assigned an errand.

He looked toward the dark entrance of the grave. Somewhere inside waited corpses, abandoned supplies, and whatever remnants of the plague-ravaged world had been forgotten below.

Alisander almost laughed.

No sound came at first.

Then a dry, rasping chuckle escaped his throat.

Apparently bureaucracy survived death as well.
◆

Magic Remembered Him

Darnell waited nearby, towering over him.

The Forsaken warrior possessed none of Alisander's hesitation. Where the mage examined the world as if trying to reconstruct a shattered puzzle, Darnell simply accepted it.

Deathknell was here.

They were here.

There was work to do.

Simple.

Alisander envied him.

Together they descended toward the Shadow Grave.

With every step, the light faded.

The smell changed too—earth, old cloth, rot, chemicals.

Things buried. Things forgotten. Things that perhaps should have remained forgotten.

Alisander lifted his hand.

Instinct answered.

At first, nothing happened.

Then something deep inside him stirred.

Not life.

Power.

A faint shimmer of arcane energy crawled across his fingertips. Blue-white light illuminated the bones beneath his damaged skin.

Alisander froze.

The sensation was familiar.

More familiar than his own face.

Magic remembered him. Even if the world had forgotten.

A smile slowly formed across the corpse's ruined features.

"There you are," he whispered.

◆

The Shadow Grave

The Shadow Grave welcomed them with silence.

Broken coffins lined the walls. Supplies lay scattered among the dead. Shadows shifted beyond the reach of Alisander's conjured light.

Darnell moved ahead.

Alisander followed.

Something scraped against stone.

He turned.

A shape moved between two coffins.

His arm rose before conscious thought could command it.

Words surfaced from a memory older than death.

Arcane structure. Intent. Release.

Energy flashed through the darkness.

For one perfect instant, Alisander was no longer a confused corpse wandering through a grave.

He was a mage.

The spell struck.

The darkness recoiled.

And something inside Alisander awakened with it.

Power did not care whether the hand commanding it was warm or cold.

The Arcane did not mourn.

It did not judge.

It demanded discipline, knowledge and will.

Those things remained his.

Perhaps they were the only parts of Alisander that truly mattered.

◆

The Question Every Corpse Faced

They continued deeper.

Embalming fluid. Twine. Supplies for the newly awakened.

Mordo's request was simple, but Alisander began to understand its meaning.

Deathknell was not merely a graveyard.

It was a beginning.

Every corpse climbing from the soil faced the same impossible question.

What now?

The living feared death because they believed it was an ending.

The Forsaken knew something worse.

Sometimes it wasn't.

Alisander had been given another existence without being asked whether he wanted one.

He could mourn.

He could rage.

He could search endlessly for the person he had once been.

Or...

He could decide what the creature standing here would become.

That possibility was terrifying.

It was also intoxicating.

◆

Beyond the Grave

When Alisander finally emerged from the Shadow Grave, the poisoned forests of Tirisfal stretched before him.

The sky remained dark.

The trees remained dead.

Nothing had become beautiful during his absence.

And yet the world looked different.

Because now he understood something.

He had awakened without a heartbeat. Without warmth. Without certainty that even his memories belonged entirely to him.

But he had awakened with his mind intact.

And somewhere beneath dead flesh and shattered memory, the mage remained.

His old life might have ended in fear.

This one would not.

Alisander looked toward the road leading beyond Deathknell.

There would be answers somewhere out there.

About his death.

About the Forsaken.

About the strange power that had dragged him back into the world.

Perhaps even about the man he had once been.

But those answers could wait.

For now, Undertaker Mordo needed his supplies.

Alisander stepped forward through the sickly grass.

Behind him stood the grave from which he had emerged.

Ahead waited Tirisfal.

Then Lordaeron.

Then the world.

For the first time since opening his dead eyes, Alisander felt something almost resembling anticipation.

The grave had already claimed him once.

It would not find him so easy to keep a second time.

And somewhere in the darkness between life and death, arcane fire began to gather around his fingers.

Alisander the mage had returned.

Whether Azeroth would consider that a blessing or a catastrophe remained to be seen.

◆

Thus begins the chronicle of Alisander, Forsaken Mage of Tirisfal.
